# Forest Bandit artwork

`mosswood.png` and `bandit.png` were created for this example using the built-in image generation tool. The original outputs are copied into this directory unchanged. `bandit.png` has a transparent alpha background. No external asset service is needed at runtime.

The small item and neighbour icons are original code-drawn pixel artwork in `../pixels.mjs`. Optional sound effects are synthesized in `../app.js`.

## Woodland prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: background artwork for a playable browser pixel-art woodland scavenging game called Forest Bandit. Create a beautiful, genuinely pixel-art top-down woodland clearing, landscape 3:2 composition, no text or UI. Large blocky pixels and limited warm nostalgic handheld-game palette, crisp pixel clusters, no antialiasing or painterly brush strokes. Soft sage and deep teal trees frame a connected walkable golden dirt trail looping through a wide central clearing. At upper left a cozy hollow tree cottage with a tiny warm amber door and a little chimney, at upper right a blue pond edged with reeds and stepping stones, at lower right a tiny ancient stone ruin, at lower left red mushrooms and fallen logs. Ample open green grass and connected paths across the middle for a little raccoon to roam. Small flowers, chunky bushes, dappled sunlight. Camera directly overhead with charming slight top-down view of tree trunks and cottage facade. Inviting, cute, richly composed but legible game map. Entire picture is one continuous full-bleed woodland environment. No people, no animals, no characters, no typography, no borders, no collage, no logos or watermark.

Generated with `transparent_background: false`.

## Raccoon prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent player character sprite for the cute pixel-art woodland scavenging browser game Forest Bandit. One single adorable small round raccoon adventurer, full body, centered, front facing with slight three-quarter view. Distinct dark raccoon eye mask, bright tiny black eyes, cute small triangular ears, soft gray fur, cream muzzle, big striped tail curving off to one side, tiny paws, small forest-green neckerchief and a warm ochre backpack visible behind shoulders. Authentic chunky pixel art as if drawn in a 48 by 48 pixel grid then enlarged with nearest-neighbour: crisp square pixel clusters, limited palette with warm charcoal outlines, no gradients, no blur, no antialiasing. Charming readable silhouette, a cheerful curious expression, standing idle. Transparent background with genuine alpha, no ground, no scenery, no shadow outside the sprite, no text, no labels, no sheet, one character only. Keep entire tail and ears within frame, make the raccoon occupy most of the image.

Generated with `transparent_background: true`.
